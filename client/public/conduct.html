Code of Conduct

PSG College of Technology • Department of MCA Mentorship Standards

1. Mutual Respect & Professionalism

ALMATRACE is built upon departmental fellowship and voluntary academic and career guidance. All participants are expected to communicate respectfully, arrive on time for agreed meetings, and maintain high professional standards.

2. Scope of Mentorship

This is an educational mentorship program. Mentors offer perspectives on career paths, technology trends, and academic projects. Mentorship does not constitute medical, legal, financial, or emergency advice, nor does it guarantee job placement or grade adjustments.

3. Safety & Escalation

Harassment, inappropriate conduct, or misuse of contact details is strictly prohibited. You can instantly block any user or submit a confidential safety report to the department coordinator directly through the application.
