Privacy Notice

Version 1.0 • October 2026 • PSG College of Technology, Department of MCA

1. What We Collect

ALMATRACE practices strict data minimization. We collect only:

  • Institutional email address for verification and account authentication.
  • Display name and optional professional details (role, headline, bio).
  • Institutional cohort and roll-number/token identifier for counterpart lineage matching.
  • Voluntary topic preferences, language, and meeting availability.

2. Privacy-Safe Defaults

  • Personal email addresses and exact roll numbers are never displayed to other students or alumni.
  • You control your profile discoverability: Discoverable, Limited (active relationships only), or Hidden.
  • Unverified accounts never appear in mentor discovery.
  • Aggregated operational metrics suppress small cohorts (< 5 members) to prevent deanonymization.

3. Your Rights & Account Deactivation

You may edit your profile and preferences at any time. You can deactivate your account from your profile settings. Deactivation safely stops all future discovery and pauses active mentorships while preserving necessary audit integrity.
