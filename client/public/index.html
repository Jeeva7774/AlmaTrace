How ALMATRACE Works

A disciplined, privacy-safe journey from registration to 1:1 guidance sessions.

1 Verification

Institutional Registration

Sign up with your college email. Alumni identities are verified by the MCA department coordinator before appearing in mentorship discovery.

2 Lineage

Token Lineage Discovery

View your batch lineage sequence. If your primary counterpart is unavailable, ALMATRACE suggests verified alumni based on shared topics — always with a clear explanation.

3 Consent

Structured Request Lifecycle

A token match is not consent to mentorship. Active relationships require mutual agreement through an explicit request with clear objectives.

4 Sessions

Scheduled Guidance Sessions

Agreed pairs arrange structured sessions with meeting agendas and video links (Google Meet or campus meetings) with local IST scheduling.

Departmental Code & Privacy Guarantee

ALMATRACE is an internal departmental platform for current MCA students and alumni of PSG College of Technology.

We strictly protect community privacy: personal email addresses and exact roll numbers are never published or shared. Mentors maintain full control over their capacity limits and availability.
