How ALMATRACE works

✓

Verified Accounts

Register with your institutional email. Alumni accounts are reviewed by a department coordinator before you appear in mentor discovery.

⟷

Token Lineage

Your roll number carries a lineage. ALMATRACE identifies your token counterpart across batches and shows you your shared history — with their consent.

⊕

Smart Fallbacks

If your primary counterpart isn't available, the system suggests other verified alumni based on shared interests and goals — always with a clear explanation.

◎

Consent First

A token match is a suggestion, not an agreement. Mentorship only begins when both people explicitly accept. You control your visibility, availability, and capacity.

Invitation-only programme

ALMATRACE is an internal departmental tool. Registration is open only to current MCA students and verified MCA alumni of PSG College of Technology.

This is a mentorship and networking platform, not a replacement for any institutional system, and does not provide academic, medical, legal, or financial advice.
